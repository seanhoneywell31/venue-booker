    How the labels work
    • Data: daily English Wikipedia page views by people (bots and crawlers excluded), from the Wikimedia Analytics API.
    • The label compares the median daily views of the latest 90 days with the same 90 days 52 weeks earlier, so weekdays line up and one viral day barely moves it.
    • Rising and Fading thresholds are set in Settings (default ±20%). They are judgment calls, not fitted values.
    • Seasonal peak: the same month had the highest average daily views, at least 25% above that year's average day, in two or more of the last three full years. Useful for timing a date; the year-over-year comparison already keeps normal seasonal swings out of the label.
    • Page views measure public attention. They are not ticket sales, and English Wikipedia under-represents some audiences.
